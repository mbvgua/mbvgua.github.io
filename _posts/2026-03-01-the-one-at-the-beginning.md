> "The limits of my language are the limits of my mind. All I know is what I have words for"
> <cite>~ Ludwig Wittgenstein</cite>

Yes, this is the obligatory "pilot episode". The one that one must always be addressed at the beginning of a new adventure, be it a show, routine, project, career path e.t.c. The one in which the world points a spotlight on you and asks "_What is this? And more so why should we care_". And if we are being honest, I'm not sure I'd be able to answer that satisfactorily.

When we were younger, we did things just for the fun of it. In time, we grew older and realized that time travel is not real(**_\*yet_**) and everything else just went downhill from there. We couldn't do things "just because" anymore. Your reasons needed reasons[^1]. They had to be quantifiable. The pros had to outweigh the cons. Profits had to made. Lives had to be changed for the net positive. Ergo why say I wouldn't be able to answer that question satisfactorily, but I'll answer it honestly; I'd like to expand the limits of my language...

How I see it, writing requires one to really understand what they are talking about. Say for example you'd like to write about compilers, you can't just write about things you have not become familiar with(well, in the age of A.I you could, but that is way besides the point I'm trying to make here). You'd have to spend months if not years really getting acquainted with the subject matter to freely and clearly talk about. Now imagine blowing this up into all these different niches, say music, art, tech and what not, the end result would be one massively improving on their lingua franca. So yeah, that is basically my aim for getting this blog up and running. If any of this makes sense to you, then welcome!
