## Introduction

I built SCOM(Service for Christ Outreach Ministry) for a home that caters to differently abled children in my area. Initially it was meant to be a static site, with minimal functionality, and I formed this preconceived notion that it would me a week at most to have it up and running, but man was I wrong. Having taken a little bit of time off of building frontend applications, my recall of basic things was hazy at best. I struggled doing things I had grown accustomed to [^1]. Also, thanks to **feature creep** I ended up doing some things I had not even thought of. However, it all worked out in the end, taking a little bit more time than I had anticipated, but I made the site [live](https://). In this article, I'll focus on some of the sections whose implementation I found ~~difficult~~interesting.
