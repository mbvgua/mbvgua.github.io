# thoughts

Chimamand Ngozi Adichie, in her harvard Address, circa 2018, says that to read a novel is to give honour to art. I could
say the same thing about code. I have foras long as I can rememberthought of coding as an art. Reading someone
else's code is honouring enough to care about it. Which makes the concept ofclosed source software even more
bizarre. Why would anyone create this masterpiece and decide to keep it in his enclosed mansion, art imo should be
shared. Freely and openely for the benefit of mankind.
